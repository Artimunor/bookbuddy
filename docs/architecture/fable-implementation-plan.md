# Beacon implementation plan

Build the 73 construction surfaces in the mobile inventory and the complete customer journey in the browser. Build the eight Later surfaces after construction. Keep them disabled by default. Ordinary push is part of construction; optional Lock Screen and ongoing status cards are the last device work and are not a prerequisite for attendance, dispatch or booking.

The [accepted architecture](mobile-tech-stack.md), [owner decisions](../decisions/2026-11-23-open-questions.md) and [Beacon inventory](../../design-fable/screens/inventory.md) are the sources for this plan. Where the architecture or a drawing predates an owner decision, use the decision: **the provider bills and collects directly, attendance never uses location, and cover is from the provider’s own crew.** English and European Portuguese are the UI locales. The fictional Xàbia job does not establish a service area.

The repository has no implemented call paths or measured application baseline. Counts below are implementation budgets. Tool and registry observations are identified separately from design decisions.

## 2. Sources of truth and D1 schema

D1 owns identity, authority, business/place/venue facts, versions, state and the durable effect ledger. Store provider contact routes, normalized site address, verified property pin, service area and IANA site zone once. Project them where needed; do not look them up at render or on attendance. Snapshot immutable contracting facts only when they are part of terms the customer accepted. R2 owns bytes, not access or approval. Mobile SQLite owns local drafts, the unsent outbox and a limited last-confirmed copy; app-owned files own pending bytes. Web holds scoped route data in memory. Catalogs own product copy; authored words remain separate. Providers own invoices and bank/PSP reconciliation; BookBuddy records an attributed outcome, not money movement.

### Conventions

- Opaque IDs are `TEXT` UUIDs or random tokens; printable `BB-*` references are separate unique labels, never credentials. Store UTC milliseconds as `INTEGER`, durations explicitly, currency as `TEXT` and EUR amounts as integer minor units. Booleans are `INTEGER CHECK (… IN (0,1))`. Wire date/time display always uses the stored IANA zone; never fall back to device zone for site time.
- Each list of state literals below is a `TEXT NOT NULL CHECK`; expiry-dependent display states may be derived from a checked UTC deadline rather than written on read. Explicit nulls are for optional values, not missing invariants. Index child FKs used in joins, active scope lists with a stable ID tie-breaker, and due queues by deadline. Add compound scope FKs where a cross-business or cross-job link would be invalid. Don’t cascade away a decision or audit trail on revocation.
- Match `apps/api/src/db/schema/<group>.ts` to the numbered handwritten SQL. The first migration sequence is below; one owner per migration. No generated SQL, migration rewrites or schema push. Evolve applied schema with expand/contract. `db/schema/index.ts` is an integrator-owned barrel.
- Use Drizzle for typed schema and ordinary reads. Use auditable bound SQL for atomic guarded commands, multi-operation sync and effects. `openDb(binding)` is request-scoped; `prepareCommand(ctx,input)` returns statements and a decoder, not a function that does another `await db`. Compose those statements into one primary D1 `batch()` where called for. Do not query for a session in middleware and repeat the check in a second database call.

### Key transition rules

- **Intake and authority:** Request needs text or a ready original voice note, an in-area verified place, a declared arrival arrangement and every retained attachment ready. A request is not a booking. An approved default foreman may receive an intake offer, but must accept it. Handoff is one named offer; incumbent remains until acceptance. Use two staffed hours normally, 30 staffed minutes plus human call for the urgent policy, then at most one authorised reoffer. No auto-assignment.
- **Property and workforce:** Verify owner/successor independently. One named approver at all times for an active place; replace in the same transaction. Ordinary removal cannot strand a last admin, owner/manager or accountable foreman. Suspension revokes immediately and opens a named operations exception with commitments paused as applicable. Provider invites: 48 hours, reissue once per 24 hours and twice per seven days. Place invites: 48 hours, five outstanding, one per recipient, two reissues per seven days and at least 15 minutes apart. Default view only, no past history. Status and expiry are visible to foremen/admins; raw eligibility proofs are not. Enforce eligibility through shift end; notify at 30 and 7 days and flag affected confirmed shifts.
- **Commercial:** Separate author, named reviewer/publisher grant and named customer approver. The author or issuer cannot approve by changing role. Lock submitted drafts and issued versions. A quote expires seven calendar days from issue; a change expires after 72 hours or earlier affected-work start. Record exact terms/version/hash, authority and time; send a durable copy. A change never silently moves a visit. No tentative holds for construction windows: recheck capacity at issue and confirmation. Revision does not change the existing window until confirmed. Fee-free pre-start cancellation; at least 24 hours ahead allow an eligible like-for-like provider-authorised alternative, otherwise request agreement. Statutory withdrawal is a separate notice and receipt, not provider permission. No implementation authority to invent binding legal wording.
- **Staffing:** Represent each required person as a slot. Ordinary named offer acceptance confirms that assignment if all checks pass. Cover issue checks same business, on duty, relevant skill, briefable language, capacity, rest/overlap, scope and expiry in one transaction; at most three recipients and two dispatches. Default reply window 15 minutes, with at least five for replies and five for review before the safe cutoff. Acceptance CAS on `(gap, round)` wins once and expires after five minutes capped by cutoff and shift start. It grants **no** travel, address, chat or customer-roster access. Confirm rechecks the same revision, role, slot and worker and inserts at most one assignment plus fresh safety hold. A losing acceptance has no write or follow-up read. On withdrawal/lapse/recheck failure terminate that acceptance round; only still-valid offers can be shown under a new acceptance epoch in the same dispatch, requiring a fresh deliberate tap. Do not replay an old tap into the new epoch or rebroadcast the round. If no safe cover, propose a revised visit; a different business requires a new agreement.
- **Live and safety:** Customer sees confirmed names plus crew-level, deliberately published facts. No candidates, individual attendance, dropout reasons, worker chat, positions or inferred arrival. Worker departure is internal unless they are the designated lead. Correct/retract by adding a stamp. Attendance during a hold is allowed and independent; require ordered arrival/departure, preserve actual/capture/receipt times and route out-of-window or disputed records to human review. The normal review ranges are 30 minutes before to 60 after start, and accepted arrival to 120 minutes after end for departure. A new instruction or replacement requires its own current teach-back and named worker acknowledgement online. Starting or resuming affected work checks the current hold; cached clearance cannot authorise it. F-13 is a log, not an editor or the employer’s statutory register.
- **Aftercare:** Completion records each approved scope item, test/result and the agreed evidence, or explicitly why not done. An unavailable view does not invent evidence; a required upload must be received or replaced by the agreed evidence alternative before submission. Sign-off is online and exact-version; no inferred acceptance. One reminder at day three and human follow-up at day seven. Problem case receipts name audience, case owner and timed targets: one business day to acknowledge, two to substantive update, then two-day updates while awaiting a plan. Case status does not rewrite acceptance or bills. A named billing authority can defer an eligible unpaid disputed amount separately. The provider alone records a matched payment, with corrections in history. Unmatched/reversed means checking and no second payment prompt. Bill due normally seven days after issue; no customer “I paid” action. Ratings private, once, within 30 calendar days of verified delivery.
- **Retention:** Unlinked upload expiry seven days; ordinary internal progress media and site attendance 90 days after close; ordinary chat/media/translation 180 days after close; minimal grant audit 12 months after end. Intentional decision records, case evidence and safety records have a separate approved schedule and per-record holds. Make expired media unavailable in projections. No gate/alarm codes or raw proof documents, even in capture prompts, translation data, pushes or cache. Use confirmed live human handoff for secrets; store only arranger and time.

## 3. API and transport

`apps/api/src/modules/<name>/{routes,read,commands}.ts` implements each group below. Contract `packages/api-contract/src/<name>.ts` defines route inputs, explicit response projections and safe conflicts; `packages/api-client/src/<name>.ts` wraps those definitions. `common.ts` defines IDs, context, pagination, evidence references and envelopes. `links.ts` owns canonical `/l/:opaque` HTTPS destinations. No write action uses a link method. `app.ts` mounts modules, `index.ts` exports fetch/queue/scheduled and the DO class.

```ts
type Result<T> =
  | { ok: true; data: T; meta: { serverTime: number; requestId: string } }
  | { ok: false; error: { code: ErrorCode; fieldErrors?: FieldError[];
      retryAt?: number; current?: SafeConflict }; meta: { requestId: string } };

type CommandInput<T> = {
  operationId: string; expectedRevision?: number; payload: T;
};

type SyncRequest = { contextId: string; cursor: string | null; operations: SyncOp[] };
type SyncPage = {
  mode: "delta" | "snapshot" | "reset";
  context: ScopeHeader; outcomes: OpOutcome[];
  upserts: Projection[]; removals: Removal[]; cursor: string; hasMore: boolean;
};
```

Use `GET` for scoped reads; `POST` for verbs and batches, `PATCH` for changed account settings; auth `401`, reason-safe `404` for scope/unknown, `409` for stale/conflict, `422` for validation, `429` for throttling, `503` for transient failure. Codes, safe details and times are locale-neutral; `Accept-Language` is a display hint on existing requests. Private responses `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. The typed client injects credentials and fetch, validates wire data, and makes **one explicit transport call**. Each app owns query keys `[contextId, resource, id, view]`; commands merge returned projections or invalidate just changed keys. No generic background refetch, retries or cache on role switch.

### Identity and write reconciliation

Email is low-assurance sign-in. Generate a random six-digit code, store only its keyed digest bound to the email and attempt, expire at ten minutes, and mark consumed once. Resend after 60 seconds, three sends total in a rolling 30 minutes per address; separate hashed device and source limits. Five wrong checks in 30 minutes lock verification and reissue for 15 minutes without affecting other sessions. Reissue invalidates the prior code, not failure history. Lockout shows viewer time. Start responds neutrally whether an account exists; status goes through the unguessable possession-bound attempt. Verify in one batch: check counters, consume, find/create account, issue session and return setup/preferences/context choices. For a lost verify response, the same verified request and possession proof may return the same session credential for a short bounded replay window, not issue another session or permit a reused code to open a different one.

Use an opaque 256-bit credential, hashed in D1; native bearer in SecureStore, web same-origin `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, `SameSite=Lax` host cookie. Require a trusted Origin for browser writes/upgrades; no credentials in query strings. Web and native never log email challenge material. Session lifetime defaults to 30 days absolute; no sliding D1 write on ordinary reads. High-impact grant activation and emergency transfer require independent operations verification by default. Passkeys can replace that step only once proven; absent a previously enrolled passkey, no lost-mailbox transfer. Verified email change revokes old sessions, audits and notifies the old mailbox. Pending invites may be withdrawn and reissued; they cannot transfer identity.

Each command guards scope, version and preconditions in SQL. Insert a receipt only if the command is eligible to change state. Guard subsequent statements with that invocation’s receipt/transition, write resource changes, audit, sync change keys and effect intents atomically, and return compact outcomes from the same batch. A duplicate matches actor/scope/input hash and reuses committed outcome; changed input for the same ID conflicts. Unauthorised replay discloses nothing. Competing cover responses return safe current status without persisting a losing claim. A committed operation stays unique even if its compact receipt is later pruned. A lost transport response is **Checking**, not success, failure or a fresh operation ID.

### Sync and realtime

- One in-flight sync per active context. Hard defaults: eight eligible operations per request, 100 changes per page, 256 KiB request cap, a bounded prepared-statement budget below the Worker/D1 limit. Operation allowlist: attendance, chat append, authorised progress attachment links once ready, and read marks. Unsent request/quote/change/case drafts stay local. A pending result from an already-submitted online-only command is looked up, not auto-executed by sync.
- Signed cursor binds scope, visibility epoch, sequence or snapshot anchor, and expiry (seven days by default). Do not write a server cursor row on every read. For a page, build the D1 batch **before** executing: scope guard, ordered idempotent operation statements, outcomes, then authorised changes/removals or snapshot rows. Empty outbox takes the delta read path. Feed rows are changed record keys, not copied payloads; read current safe projections once. Capture a high-water mark and use keyset pagination. A snapshot pins its initial feed anchor; changes after the anchor catch late inserts and edits even if their key falls behind the snapshot page.
- Bump the affected context visibility epoch for a grant change that invalidates its cached projections. On mismatched epoch or revoked scope, hide/remove affected data **before** rendering, then reconcile a scoped snapshot if still entitled. Never filter away the only revocation information because its record is no longer readable. Apply page, removals, acknowledgements and cursor in one SQLite transaction. On failure preserve cursor and IDs; retry only at the next eligible foreground/reconnect/explicit action.
- Use one hibernating `LiveTopic` DO class, namespaced by opaque topic: customer visit, provider job/crew, scope inbox, exact thread. A cold thread upgrade does scope and bounded catch-up in **one D1 read inside the DO route**; deliver it as the first frame, no duplicate HTTP loader. Web uses the session cookie; native supplies the auth header. Socket attachment stores only the checked scope and a short lease. Hints contain a scoped cursor/invalidation, no text, position, address or attendee information. Every subsequent scoped read reauthorises. Close affected subscriptions on revoke when publishing to those topics; stale/expired sockets have no authority. Foreground clients coalesce hints; no polling or background socket. The publisher does not query presence: one publish attempt per distinct changed relevant topic, with fan-out only to current sockets. The idle-topic attempt is counted, not hidden as a free presence lookup.

### Media, effects and providers

**Media.** Stage app-owned bytes before local receipt; keep stable attachment ID, caller, expected size/MIME and transfer identity. The API uses built-in Web Crypto for scoped R2 signatures, not a credential per client. For small objects use short-lived conditional PUT grants (`If-None-Match: *` signed), safe MIME, size bound and private/no-store metadata. For multipart reserve once, create only missing sessions under a lease, record session IDs together, sign requested missing parts together. No JS/API byte relay. Settle claims an authorized reservation generation, skips ready IDs, verifies metadata/parts and commits all newly ready acknowledgements in one final batch. If upload or completion may have succeeded, reconcile the known key/session before another transfer; never replace settled bytes. No public bucket URLs. Signed GET after a scoped check; reuse until expiry for the active view, no persistent received-media cache.

Caller caps: request 5 photos + 1 voice; chat 3 photos **or** 1 voice; worker progress 6 photos per shift; quote/change 5 photos + 1 voice per version; completion 8 photos; problem report/update 5 photos + 1 voice. Photos ≤5 MiB, new captures up to 2,048 px long edge by default, strip metadata before staging; voice ≤2 MiB and 90 s. Cap pending bytes at 100 MiB per account/device. No customer-facing video; the transfer spike uses one 720p/20 s/20 MiB clip. Browsers use File/Blob direct uploads, remove photo metadata, and support typed input when microphone permission or encoding is unavailable.

**Effects.** Bindings `DB`, `EVIDENCE`, `LIVE`, `EFFECTS`; no KV, application Cache API or vendor polling. State change inserts unique outbox intents, not vendor sends. After commit, one Queue wake for the batch and one hint per changed topic using `waitUntil`; Queue payload carries only work keys. Consumer claims bounded due work with fencing and returns all authorized inputs, installation tokens and recipient locales in that call. At most one result batch. A due-work sweep recovers missed wakes and handles deadlines/reminders; it does not re-enqueue claimed work. An expired in-flight lease with unknown vendor outcome becomes `unknown` rather than repeating a paid mail, push or translation. Definite failures keep the original data and an actionable safe status.

**Push.** Expo HTTP send and receipts via `fetch`, 100 notices per send batch, up to 1,000 tickets per receipt batch. Localize generic titles/body from saved UI locale. Payload includes only an opaque destination; token is stored and updated on change only. Disable the exact returned dead token epoch, not a replacement installation. Receipts are transport results, not read marks. Use one scheduled receipt pass for due tickets, not per-ticket polling. Workers with missed/denied notices still have W-03, F-10 and the authorised duty line.

**Translation.** The accepted suggestion selects **Google Cloud Translation Advanced, EU endpoint, pre-trained NMT**, subject to processor/security approval. Use `TranslationProvider.translate(pair, texts, credential)` over HTTP. Do not substitute Workers AI on the strength of having a Cloudflare account. Deploy with pairs disabled until reviewed; initial candidates Spanish↔English, Spanish↔German/Romanian/Arabic. Any Portuguese reading pair is separately approved, not implied by Portuguese UI. Require 50 de-identified snippets per direction, ≥90% usable without correction and no critical meaning changes; do not label Darija as Arabic without separate review.

On append, one SQL transaction derives distinct required authorised targets from saved preferences and inserts only missing revision/target intents. Skip original-only, same-language, unknown or unapproved pairs. Preserve original and corrections. Send only eligible ordinary text with approved minimisation: strip known names and unnecessary identifiers, reject suspected access, payment, personal or restricted content when it cannot be safely redacted. Redaction is not a proof that arbitrary free text is safe; until that policy passes review, use originals and human clarification. Never send safety originals, audio, images, coordinates or access notes. Provider-reviewed quote/change reading aids are labelled and attached to the exact version; safety reading aids are human. Do not backfill history on a setting change. Claim loads the unexpired encrypted OAuth credential once; only if translation work exists and it is stale may one claimant refresh it, storing the result in the same effect result batch. Competing claimants defer without OAuth calls.

**Mail.** `MailDelivery.send(batch)` serves OTP, invitation and durable contractual receipts. Dev logs a redacted delivery event only; known synthetic test codes are configured only in an isolated local fixture, and tests can capture delivery in memory. Never print real or generated codes, addresses or invite secrets. Production default is Postmark’s HTTP batch adapter, using deployment-configured origin, sender and token after mail/privacy approval. Store short-lived delivery secrets encrypted until claim; do not leave them in queue payloads or logs. A confirmed write may say dispatch was requested, not that the recipient received it. Receipt email contains the exact accepted terms or the exact notice and receipt, not a mutable view link alone.

## 8. Assumptions and decisions still required

**Implementation assumptions:** Use explicit role grants rather than inferring powers from membership. Prefer single current customer approver, explicit handoffs even for owner-operators, structured provider terms, local operations for exceptional authority, and manual place verification for ambiguous addresses. Default P-01 bill action upon an authorised bill requiring attention; never advertise support or response targets until staffed. Use no public guest job information, no unapproved category details, and no background work triggered merely by viewing a record. Use injected time for tests only. Use the accepted six-day/visit progression semantics as **six journey steps and five visit steps**, not single mutable stage fields that can erase orthogonal conditions.

**Before production, not blockers to starting implementation:** The owner and relevant operators must settle actual catchment/coverage, provider onboarding, duty contacts, contractual and withdrawal wording, approved retention holds/schedules, processor terms for production mail and translation, bilingual review and enabled reading pairs, supported signed fleet and device evidence. Outside-crew staffing needs a specific lawful route signed off by counsel; no owner guess or UI flag bypasses it. The adult Pilates category needs an approved operator and category policy. Pricing remains a separate provider fee hypothesis: first 30 completed paid jobs free, then €25 plus VAT for eligible sourced, signed-off, paid jobs of at least €150 pre-VAT under accepted trial terms.
