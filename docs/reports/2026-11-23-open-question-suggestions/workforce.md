# Workforce decisions for the Costa Blanca pilot

These are operating suggestions, not legal advice. The thresholds below are starting defaults, not statutory limits, measured results or service guarantees. Sources were checked on 23 November 2026 unless dated separately.

**Start with about five verified local trade businesses in one catchment.** Each provider contracts directly with its customer, sends its own lawfully employed crew, and directs and pays that crew. BookBuddy introduces the business, provides scheduling software and helps people keep the booking on track. It does not supply people, undertake the build or decide who works. Launch with own-team cover, a phone fallback, human safety briefings and manual attendance. A realistic fallback is an honest revised visit, not an unverified worker.

## Operating model and lawful cover

### 1. Who is responsible for the repair and crew?

**Question:** Is BookBuddy an introducer, booking agent, employer, staffing agency or contractor? [Company brief][brief]; P-02, P-04, F-03–F-07, F-10–F-12, C-06, C-08, C-09.

**Suggested answer:** Use an introduction and booking-coordination agreement without authority to bind either party, plus provider scheduling software. Start with a counsel-approved list of straightforward, single-provider repairs, such as adjusting a non-powered door where the site assessment finds no specialist or unusual hazard. Keep structural work, hazardous work, regulated installs and multi-contractor jobs out until cleared. The named provider issues the scope and quote, contracts with and invoices the owner directly, directs the method and crew through its own foreman, and is responsible for safety, pay, Social Security, timekeeping and rectification. The owner agrees the outcome and visit and arranges access, not the crew's work. BookBuddy can relay messages and chase a stalled booking; it cannot choose individuals, set wages, assign tasks or undertake delivery in its own name. Check actual practice as well as the signed terms.

**Why:** This preserves one named contact without putting BookBuddy in the construction or labour-supply chain. The distinction fails if BookBuddy starts directing workers or sells the work itself, even if the provider name remains on screen.

**Alternatives:** Pure referral with no follow-through; disclosed booking agency with a defined mandate; managed contractor or employer only under a separately approved model.

**Evidence:** [Design roles][roles], [market beachhead][beachhead] and [competitor operating boundaries][competition]; [Workers’ Statute art. 1.1][et1] (23 October 2015) and [Ley 32/2006 art. 3][construction] (18 October 2006). Liability and classification for BookBuddy's actual contracts are [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Spanish employment and construction counsel; local operator and insurer. Have them review the job list, contracts, quote, app permissions and what support staff will say and do before accepting live work.

**Confidence:** High.

### 2. When would this become platform work or employment?

**Question:** Which actual controls and forthcoming national rules affect employment status? [Company brief][brief], [attendance research][attlaw]; P-04, F-07, F-10–F-12, W-03, W-06, W-08.

**Suggested answer:** Have the provider's named manager select recipients and confirm cover in its own roster. Use eligibility facts, expiry and overlap checks to prevent invalid actions, not automated ranking, auto-assignment, attendance scoring, wage setting or penalties for declined/missed offers. Keep a human correction/review route for gates. Ask counsel specifically whether rules supporting access to offers, the first-valid-reply reservation, or any future monitoring meet the definition of automated decision-making or monitoring; a final human tap does not by itself take a system out of scope. Recheck before any move into individual job matching, worker pricing, GPS monitoring or performance-based access.

**Why:** Directive 2024/2831 art. 2 requires a service at least partly electronic, at the recipient's request, that necessarily and essentially organises paid individual work and uses automated monitoring or decision-making. Arts. 3–5 look at actual work and protect workers through intermediaries too; facts indicating direction and control trigger a **rebuttable** employment presumption under the national rules, not automatic reclassification of every provider. Spain already applies the ordinary employment test, and the Rider Law's specific presumption concerns delivery, not construction; its separate art. 64.4(d) representative-information rule is not limited to delivery.

**Alternatives:** Provider-only scheduling without the booking layer; or a reviewed digital labour platform with the required employment and algorithmic safeguards.

**Evidence:** [Directive (EU) 2024/2831][pwd] (23 October 2024, arts. 1–5, 7–11, 29; recitals 19–20, 39); [Ley 12/2021][rider] (28 September 2021); [Workers’ Statute arts. 1, 8, 64][et1]. Transposition is due **2 December 2026**. Spain opened a [preliminary consultation][consult] on **31 July–14 August 2026**; that is not enactment. No enacted Spanish transposition was established in the official sources consulted ([EUR-Lex notifications][nim]); final Spanish text/status and its application to BookBuddy are [unverified]. If in scope, algorithmic data protections can apply even to people genuinely self-employed: arts. 7–11 cover prohibited data uses, a DPIA with worker input, transparency/data portability, human oversight and review, and human decisions for suspension or equivalent detriment.

**Confirm with:** Spanish employment and privacy counsel; verify BOE again before launch and have counsel walk through the actual screens, employment relationships and decision rules.

**Confidence:** Medium.

### 3. When is outside help labour placement or temporary agency work?

**Question:** Who can confirm replacement cover and which external staffing routes can be used? [Design review FB2][staffing], [architecture][arch], [company brief][brief]; F-09–F-12, F-16, W-03, P-04, C-09.

**Suggested answer:** Keep F-11's individual-worker search and offers off in the pilot. Only the accountable provider's authorised foreman confirms a named replacement in F-12, and only from that provider's own eligible crew. If the provider needs to hire, it hires directly or uses a formal placement partner. Matching jobseekers to employers or running external personnel selection is potentially *intermediación laboral*: an *agencia de colocación* files a *declaración responsable* with the public employment service for its principal establishment (LABORA if in the Comunitat Valenciana) and cannot charge workers for placement. Temporary supply is different: an authorised *empresa de trabajo temporal* (ETT) employs and pays the worker and contracts with the eligible user company, which directs the work and has site safety duties. Do not use an ETT in this pilot. Revisit only for a named business user, lawful qualifying temporary assignment, permitted task and reviewed allocation of safety/training and employment duties.

**Why:** Recruiting someone into employment is not the same as hiring them out; a placement filing is not an ETT authorisation. A services invoice does not cure an unlawful transfer where the arrangement merely supplies people, the supplier lacks real organisation or means, or it does not exercise employer functions.

**Alternatives:** Provider contracts directly with a qualified ETT for an expressly permitted use after review; a true subcontract for a defined result is a different route (see next question).

**Evidence:** [Ley 3/2023 arts. 40–43, 45][placement] (28 February 2023), [LABORA filing][labora], [Workers’ Statute art. 43][et43] (23 October 2015), [Ley 14/1994 arts. 1–2, 6, 8, 11–12, 15–16 and additional provision 2][ett] (1 June 1994), [Directive 2008/104/EC][agency-eu] (19 November 2008). Spanish law excludes specified especially hazardous work and allows construction restrictions under the applicable collective agreement; ETT use is not blanket-permitted or blanket-banned by sector. [Competitor staffing profiles][players] show operating models, not locally available lawful trade cover [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Spanish labour counsel, LABORA if pursuing placement, and the relevant labour authority/ETT and prevention service before enabling any ETT use. Verify the applicable construction agreement for each task and user company.

**Confidence:** High.

### 4. Can a backup business take a construction job?

**Question:** What trade, insurance, construction-chain and posting gates apply when another business is involved? [Design review FB2][staffing], [company brief][brief]; P-04, F-03, F-10–F-12, C-08, C-09.

**Suggested answer:** If own-team cover fails, the provider proposes a revised visit. If it cannot deliver the work, BookBuddy can introduce a separately verified local business for a **new** owner–provider agreement once the original commitment is resolved; never silently switch the contractor in C-09 or mix two crews under one foreman. Do not rely on posted crews, sole-trader shift cover or on-site subcontracting chains in the pilot. If later allowed, the provider may subcontract a defined unit of work, not borrow a worker: require a written scope, the subcontractor's own means and supervision, current insurance and required trade permissions, and a checked chain, REA evidence and construction safety documentation before deployment. The original contractor remains accountable for its contract; its foreman does not become the other company's line manager.

**Why:** Ley 32/2006 includes repair and maintenance on covered construction works; job size does not decide coverage. For a covered chain, check REA and the statutory subcontracting limits, verification duties and *Libro de Subcontratación*; an autónomo without employees is generally REA-exempt but normally cannot subcontract onward, and an autónomo with employees on the work is treated as a contractor/subcontractor. REA is neither a trade licence nor site clearance.

**Alternatives:** A vetted genuine subcontract after site-specific approval; no out-of-provider work and only rebook/reschedule if contracting clarity cannot be achieved.

**Evidence:** [Ley 32/2006 arts. 2–8, 10][construction] (18 October 2006), [RD 1109/2007][rea-rd] (24 August 2007), [Ministry REA FAQ][rea-faq] and [Valencian REA lookup][rea-gva]; [Workers’ Statute art. 42][et42] for applicable business contracting duties; [RD 1627/1997][site-law] (24 October 1997) and [RD 171/2004][cae] (30 January 2004) for prevention coordination. [Ley 45/1999][posting] (29 November 1999) and [EU posting guidance][posting-eu] distinguish host conditions, declarations and A1; A1 is not immigration or trade clearance. [Market legal notes][markets]. Specific permit, insurance, promoter and coordination obligations are job-dependent [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Alicante construction counsel, provider's prevention service and insurer. Check REA at onboarding and applicable project-specific certification before work (RD 1109/2007 art. 6 and Ministry FAQ §1.8); confirm municipal/trade permissions and responsibilities of the owner/promoter, provider and any safety coordinator.

**Confidence:** High.

### 5. What makes a worker or backup provider deployable?

**Question:** What should the pilot verify, and how will it learn what replacement supply actually exists? [Company brief][brief], [design review FB2/P3][staffing]; P-01, P-04, F-07–F-12, F-16, W-03, C-09.

**Suggested answer:** Local operations maintains a directory of roughly **five** vetted businesses, not a cross-employer worker pool. Verify legal entity and employer identity, authorised foreman and backup lead, REA when required, work-scope/trade permissions, prevention arrangements, and current liability cover for that scope. The employer verifies identity, employment and Social Security registration, right to work in the exact role where required, competence and job-specific prevention training. Use an authorised reviewer to check the minimum authoritative evidence, not a self-checked badge. Confirm availability across the full visit, realistic travel/rest time and an attainable briefing for each shift. Start with **one qualified lead and one identified eligible alternate per offered trade within the provider** before describing that provider as having backup capacity; otherwise take planned bookings only with a clear no-cover fallback. Run a short dropout drill with each provider before accepting time-sensitive jobs. Over eight operating weeks, log every real gap by trade, locality and notice (<1 h, 1–4 h, >4 h), along with valid candidates, actual on-time qualified arrival, no-cover and reason. Review weekly; fewer than **20 actual comparable gaps** is too little even to discuss a public fill-rate target, and a larger sample is not a guarantee.

**Why:** Registered workers, invitations and provisional replies do not tell the owner if anyone can safely turn up. This measures the local constraint without claiming availability from employment statistics or competitors' coverage pages.

**Alternatives:** One anchor provider for a narrower set of trades; commission an external supplier only after the route and its observed coverage pass separate checks.

**Evidence:** [Company team and entry model][company-teams], [market supply and posting notes][workforce-market], [competitor limits][competitor-limits]; [LO 4/2000 art. 36][immigration] (11 January 2000), [RD 1155/2024][permits] (19 November 2024, as amended), [Ley 31/1995 arts. 18–19][prl] (8 November 1995), and [Valencian low-voltage installer register procedure][electrical] as an example of a trade-specific check. Local trade liquidity, safety language coverage and fill rate are [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Local operator, participating employers, Spanish labour/immigration adviser and insurer; use pilot arrivals and drills, not desk estimates.

**Confidence:** Medium.

### 6. What is visible, and what happens if eligibility expires?

**Question:** What can foremen and admins see, when should they be alerted, and who handles a confirmed shift crossing a new expiry? [Design review FA3/P3][staffing]; F-07–F-12, F-16, P-01, P-04, S-09.

**Suggested answer:** Show the foreman only task/site fit, working/briefing language, last check and the effective cutoff with coarse outcomes: approved, expiring, pending, expired, suspended, or not approved for this task. Show the provider admin the same plus its own business/category scope, safe label for the check needing action, renewal date and secure support route. Make raw documents, numbers and immigration/medical/disciplinary reasons unavailable to both in the app; only authorised reviewers use the secure evidence route. Keep a verification record (scope, outcome, reviewer/date, cutoff or review-by date, evidence pointer if necessary); leave underlying employee proofs with the employer where possible. Alert admin **30 and 7 calendar days** before expiry and the foreman only when an assigned job is affected; for truly undated checks set a review-by date of **90 days**, not a fictitious expiry. Require the shift to end before the effective cutoff. On a newly reported lapse or suspension, block new offers immediately and flag existing affected shifts to the employer's accountable foreman and local compliance lead. They check evidence and stop or restaff affected work before it can proceed ineligible, without silently cancelling the customer's booking. If a worker is already on site, report actual time worked and do not erase history. Notify the worker privately and offer a human review/correction.

**Why:** A business approval, work authorisation, site qualification and availability are different facts. Showing the consequence and cutoff lets the foreman act without distributing the underlying personal records.

**Alternatives:** Admin-only status with support rechecking every shift; or more disclosure only for a specific legally authorised role and need.

**Evidence:** [Provider P-04][providers], [foreman F-09 and F-16][foreman-cover], [roles][roles]; [GDPR][gdpr] (27 April 2016, arts. 5, 6, 25, 32). The proposed alert and review periods are not legal periods.

**Confirm with:** Provider admin, local compliance lead, Spanish labour/privacy adviser and affected foremen; test one expiry crossing and one correction with each employer.

**Confidence:** High.

## Booking and staffing decisions

### 7. Is a proposed visit held, and what staffing check is needed?

**Question:** Should F-06 hold tentative capacity, and how do assessment and work visits differ? [Design review FA2/W3][staffing]; F-06, F-07, C-08, W-03, W-04, W-08.

**Suggested answer:** No tentative hold or assignment lock in construction. Show “Capacity checked, not reserved”; recheck at send and when the customer agrees, and record the confirmed provider window only if still available. Use a proposal deadline no later than the end of the next staffed day or the provider's safe staffing cutoff, whichever is earlier; do not beg the customer to race a disappearing slot. An assessment is inspection/scoping only: one authorised, competent assessor with site access and a briefing appropriate to the visit's actual hazards. No repair starts on an assessment booking. A work visit needs approved scope, the proper provider, checked provider capacity for all required skills, and a verified way to brief the crew before work; check each named worker on offer/confirmation for the whole shift. `Visit booked`, `Crew confirmed` and `Cleared to start` remain separate. If the window loses capacity before confirmation, leave any existing visit unchanged and offer a new proposal.

**Why:** One simple rule prevents speculative proposals from blocking the schedule. Rechecking protects the agreed visit, while avoiding any claim that a visit is staffed or safe merely because it is booked.

**Alternatives:** A short capacity hold for a high-demand work type only after measured loss rates justify it; confirm only after all crew accept if unstaffed bookings prove too costly.

**Evidence:** [F-06/F-07 design][foreman-intake], [core booking and staffing flow][flows], [accepted architecture][arch]; [Ley 31/1995 arts. 16, 18–19][prl] (8 November 1995) for risk and training duties.

**Confirm with:** Pilot providers and scheduling data: proposal collisions, unstaffed bookings and resulting moves; prevention lead approves assessment limits.

**Confidence:** Medium.

### 8. Who can change availability or report overlap?

**Question:** Can workers and businesses mark unavailability over a confirmed assignment? [Design review FA2/W3][staffing]; W-03, W-04, W-05, W-08, F-07–F-10, F-16.

**Suggested answer:** Let workers edit **their own future offer availability**, including declaring unavailability over a confirmed shift with a clear warning and a separate `Can't attend` route. This suppresses new offers but never cancels or replaces an assignment. Let an explicitly delegated employer scheduler record a visible, attributed business availability correction (for instance recorded leave); a foreman can inspect conflicts and route them, not silently overwrite the worker's declaration. If the worker reports a confirmed conflict directly by phone, the foreman records who reported it and the operational gap as the foreman, not a fabricated worker action. At offer and confirmation, the employer checks actual overlap, travel and employment rules; no override to assign into a confirmed conflict.

**Why:** A declaration is a planning signal, while opening a booked gap is a consequential action. Separating them preserves existing commitments and still lets a worker alert the provider to a real problem.

**Alternatives:** Worker-only edits with employer-managed conflicts by phone; delegate attributed availability changes to the foreman where the provider has no separate scheduler.

**Evidence:** [W-05/W-08 design][worker], [role boundaries][roles], [core flows][flows]; [Workers’ Statute arts. 20 bis, 34–35][et34] (23 October 2015) for disconnection, working time and rest.

**Confirm with:** Providers and workers; employer counsel checks working-hours, schedule-notice and collective-agreement constraints.

**Confidence:** High.

### 9. When does a foreman intake or transfer offer expire?

**Question:** Who sets the named handoff deadline and handles a stalled offer? [Design review P2/FA2][staffing], [P-02/F-03 specs][providers]; P-02, F-03, S-09, S-16, S-17.

**Suggested answer:** Local operations sets the common policy; the provider admin selects the named recipient and sees the deadline before send. Start with **two staffed hours** (Monday–Friday 08:00–18:00 Spain time, excluding local public holidays). For an at-risk same-day handoff due within four staffed hours, set **30 staffed minutes** and call the recipient; if any wait would put the visit at risk, call the provider's duty line immediately. Allow only one live handoff to one foreman. On decline/expiry reoffer once if an authorised alternate exists, otherwise escalate to the provider admin and local desk. An authorised incumbent stays responsible until a transfer is confirmed; stalled first intake is not a booking.

**Why:** Long enough to read the record, short enough that an owner request does not sit under the wrong team. A deadline is an operating promise only if someone monitors it.

**Alternatives:** One business day for non-urgent intake; direct phone triage only for urgent transfers.

**Evidence:** [P-02 rules][providers], [F-03 rules][foreman-intake], [accountable handoffs][flows]. Pilot foreman response times and actual desk coverage are [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Local desk and provider admins; review missed handoffs and urgent calls weekly before publishing any target.

**Confidence:** Medium.

### 10. How long should provider invitations last?

**Question:** Who sets the provider invitation-link lifetime and resend limits? [Design review P2][staffing]; P-02, P-05, S-05, S-17. This is not the sign-in code or property-delegation policy.

**Suggested answer:** BookBuddy fixes policy across pilot providers; authorised provider admins can send, withdraw or reissue a known person's invitation but not lengthen it. Use **48 hours**, single-use, bound to the invited verified address, business and grants. Reissue at most once per **24 hours** and **twice per rolling 7 days** per address/business; a successful reissue invalidates the old link and starts its own 48 hours. An authorised sender corrects a wrong mailbox by withdrawing and re-inviting, not transferring identity. Membership expiry stays separate and accepting membership creates no eligibility or assignment.

**Why:** Two days is usable for a small team's onboarding without a permanent access link. Reissue limits avoid repeated spam and competing invitations without assigning any extra worker rights.

**Alternatives:** Seven-day links for low-frequency cohorts; tighter links with assisted onboarding if business risk requires it.

**Evidence:** [P-05 open questions and workflow][providers], [identity/invite contract][ia], [provider/worker separation][roles]. No pilot mail-delivery rate is known [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Pilot provider admins and the product/identity owner; monitor expiry and legitimate reissue rates.

**Confidence:** Medium.

### 11. How long are worker offers open, and who can cut a round short?

**Question:** Who sets worker reply expiry, batch size and own-team urgency cutoff? [Design review FA2/FB1/FB2][staffing], [architecture][arch]; F-06, F-07, F-10–F-12, W-03, S-09.

**Suggested answer:** Local operations sets bounds; the provider's authorised foreman chooses recipients, not BookBuddy. Start with **one** named ordinary offer per slot, open for **two of the recipient's scheduled working hours**, capped by a safe confirmation cutoff. For urgent internal cover send to at most **three** eligible people already on duty per round; default **15 minutes** for replies and **one further round** if time permits. Aim to staff it at least **20 minutes before start** or earlier if travel and briefing need more time. Allow at least **5 minutes** to reply and leave **5 minutes** for review within that safe deadline; if impossible, use the provider duty line or revise the visit. The accountable foreman or designated provider backup can end a round early for a recorded delivery or safety reason; close outstanding offers before any new round, never bump a valid provisional acceptance, and never unlock an unapproved external route. No routine off-duty chasing or penalty for missing/declining an optional offer. Apply employment and rest rules even if someone taps Accept.

**Why:** Small named batches are quick to manage without creating a broadcast labour market. A response timer measures how long an offer is open, not whether the employer lawfully changed a schedule.

**Alternatives:** Sequential calls to one worker at a time; widen a batch only if measured expiry and fill results justify it.

**Evidence:** [F-07/F-10/F-12][foreman-cover], [W-03][worker], [core cover rules][flows], [Connecteam/Planday profiles][players]; [Workers’ Statute arts. 20 bis, 34–35][et34] and [LOPDGDD art. 88][disconnect] (5 December 2018). The statute's five-day notice for irregular distribution under art. 34.2 is not replaced by an app deadline.

**Confirm with:** Providers and counsel, including collective-agreement notice and disconnection; tune using actual reply/travel times, including missed pushes.

**Confidence:** Medium.

### 12. How long is a provisional place held, and when is the owner told?

**Question:** Who confirms cover, when does the hold end and who owns the next customer update? [Design review FB1/FB2][staffing], [architecture][arch]; F-10–F-12, F-16, W-03, W-04, S-09, C-09.

**Suggested answer:** Policy gives **five minutes after the first server-recorded valid reply** for the provider's foreman or pre-authorised backup to confirm once, capped by the earlier safe staffing deadline and never at or after shift start. Worker reply expiry and this review expiry are separate. Recheck authority, eligibility, overlap and current assignment; a changed slot invalidates the prior review. Pending means no travel/access or customer roster update. On withdrawal, failed recheck or timeout keep the gap open and only reopen still-valid offers to deliberate responses. If delivery is at risk the named foreman sends C-09 an update immediately, with the unchanged agreed window, no provisional names, and the next update **within 15 minutes or by visit start if sooner**. If start has passed, give the next 15-minute checkpoint or a confirmed plan. If cover is not safe by the cutoff, tell the owner and use the revised-visit flow, not a silent time change.

**Why:** The server can preserve a single place while a human checks the real situation. The owner gets a useful update even when nothing can yet be promised.

**Alternatives:** A longer review window for non-urgent cover; phone triage and no timed app round when there is no safe decision time.

**Evidence:** [Core replacement flow][flows], [one-acceptance architecture][arch], [F-12/F-16][foreman-cover] and [customer C-09][customer-day]. Five and 15 minutes are proposed operator targets [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Accountable foremen, desk staffing and pilot dropout drills including a missed notice, timeout and unconfirmed competing reply.

**Confidence:** Medium.

### 13. What human channel actually answers?

**Question:** What covers stalled handoffs, no cover and urgent unsent dropouts? What can an assigned worker use offline and a candidate use before assignment? [Design review P2, FB2, W2, SB3][staffing], [architecture][arch]; P-02, F-10, F-11, W-03–W-06, W-09, S-11, S-16, L-06.

**Suggested answer:** Staff one local operations phone plus S-16 support form **08:00–18:00 Spain time on local business days**, with a primary and a named backup; book only visits within staffed coverage. Aim to return an urgent delivery call in **10 minutes** and a routine message within **two staffed hours**, without advertising either until staffed and rehearsed. The provider supplies an assigned worker with a verified **work number** reaching the foreman or duty backup, accessible without app data and on a printed job card if needed. Do not cache residential access details or private numbers. Before assignment use the provider's public/work dispatch number or S-16-mediated contact, with the opaque offer reference and no property or customer details. For a failed urgent W-05 send display `Foreman not notified`; call the work line now, then backup/local desk if not answered within **five minutes**. A telephone report becomes a server event only when the authorised actor logs it. Outside staffed hours use the labelled next opening, not a 24/7 desk promise; a live booked visit must have employer backup. For immediate danger, stop work and use site emergency procedures/112; BookBuddy support is not an emergency service.

**Why:** An app alert is not a receipt, and a phone route can work without mobile data. Assigning an actual duty owner avoids relying on workers’ personal numbers or unstaffed after-hours promises.

**Alternatives:** Providers handle every urgent call on their own swap line; add a funded evening/weekend rota only with demonstrated demand and employment/disconnection checks.

**Evidence:** [S-16][shared], [W-05/W-09][worker], [notification rules][ia], [112 Comunitat Valenciana][emergency] and [LOPDGDD art. 88][disconnect] (5 December 2018). Duty-line staffing, coverage and response targets are [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Local operator and every provider; rehearse a missed push, voice-only contact, absent foreman and shift that overruns staffed hours.

**Confidence:** Medium.

## Safety and attendance

### 14. What counts as understanding a safety briefing?

**Question:** Which spoken languages, human briefings and explain-back records qualify, including Arabic and Romanian? [Design review W4/SB2][safety], [architecture][arch], [company brief][brief]; F-07, F-10–F-13, F-16, W-03, W-09, S-10, S-11.

**Suggested answer:** The employer/prevention lead defines task-specific hazards, required training, what needs demonstration and the briefing language or interpreter. Use Spanish when the assigned person can actually work safely in Spanish. Provision one named, prevention-briefed human contact and backup for any other language offered: ask for actual spoken preference (e.g. Darija vs another Arabic variety, or Romanian), use a competent bilingual foreman or qualified interpreter, and verify task communication for ongoing hazards as well as induction. Do not accept jobs depending on Arabic, Romanian or another language until that cover is booked and rehearsed; local availability is [unverified]. Before affected work, the worker explains or demonstrates back **the hazard, the control and what to do or whom to call if it changes**. Record worker, job and instruction version, human verifier, language/interpreter if used, time and checklist outcome; keep original text, any reviewed translation and linked revisions in the private scope, with no routine audio recording. The worker acknowledges the exact verified version online; no machine translation, read receipt, click alone or attendance suffices. On no signal, failed understanding, a material revision or a replacement, the work stays held until the required new acknowledgment and any site clearance. Do not withhold the ability to record attendance.

**Why:** The employer owes understandable risk information and adequate theoretical and practical training; an app acknowledgment documents one step, not a qualification or a substitute for the employer's duties. A language label is not evidence of comprehension.

**Alternatives:** Book a qualified on-site interpreter for selected visits; defer tasks or use an eligible crew with a shared working language if a safe briefing cannot be arranged.

**Evidence:** [S-11 and W-09][shared], [core safety flow][flows], [market language evidence][market-language], [ELA research][ela] (2025) and [INSST NTP 825][insst] (2009). [Ley 31/1995 arts. 18–21, 24][prl] (8 November 1995), [Ley 32/2006 art. 10][construction] (18 October 2006), and [RD 1627/1997][site-law] (24 October 1997).

**Confirm with:** Each provider's occupational prevention service, workers/representatives and named interpreters; approve translated field terms and observe teach-back for each actual language/task combination.

**Confidence:** High.

### 15. Is job attendance the Spanish working-time register?

**Question:** What does W-06 record, who owns the *registro de jornada* and who may correct either record? [Design review W1][safety], [attendance research][attlaw]; W-04, W-06, F-13, F-16.

**Suggested answer:** Run W-06 as **job-site arrival/departure only**. Each employer keeps its existing compliant register for employees' actual daily start/end and any other working-time detail needed to measure time worked (breaks, work travel, training, waiting as applicable); never autofill it from scheduled windows, boundaries, or elapsed site minutes. Organise the register through collective bargaining or company agreement or, failing that, employer decision after consulting legal worker representatives where applicable. Keep those records **four years**, accessible to the worker, their legal representatives and labour inspectors. For W-06, keep actual event time and capture/receipt times distinct; record queued events in order and never show a confirmation that has not reconciled. A worker can submit a manual event or request a correction, including without a suitable personal phone. If needed an authorised foreman records an **assisted site event** under their own name, and only an authorised employer timekeeper corrects the statutory register. Link original and amended actual times, author, reason and correction time; notify the worker and allow dispute. Keep F-13 read-only with the visible correction, no impersonation. Do not make approval of a site correction a condition of recording actual work or of wages.

**Why:** Starting work, arriving at a site and being cleared to work can occur at different times. A genuine independent provider does not become an employee obliged to clock into an employer register merely for recording arrival, and its own employees still require their employer’s register.

**Alternatives:** A later employer-owned statutory module after approval, with BookBuddy acting only on documented instructions under a GDPR art. 28 agreement for that purpose.

**Evidence:** [W-06][worker], [F-13][foreman-cover], [offline architecture][arch]; [ET art. 34.9][et34], added by [RD-ley 8/2019 art. 10][timelaw] (8 March 2019), and [CJEU C-55/18 para. 60][cjeu] (14 May 2019); [AEPD employment guide pp. 35–36][aepd] (updated December 2025). The separate [Ministry draft decree][time-draft] does not itself mandate digital records; final publication/requirements are [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Employer, payroll/timekeeping lead and Spanish labour adviser; check current BOE and the applicable agreement at go-live, and verify workers can record, see and correct time without a phone or GPS.

**Confidence:** High.

### 16. Where should a site boundary apply, and what evidence survives?

**Question:** Which sites, accuracy, schedule windows and location retention should W-06 use? [Design review W1][safety], [architecture][arch], [attendance research][attdesign]; W-04, W-06, F-13.

**Suggested answer:** **No location requirement or boundaries at launch.** Deliberate location-free attendance is the default. Use scheduled times only as review prompts: arrival from **30 minutes before to 60 minutes after** booked start, departure from recorded arrival to **120 minutes after** booked end. Outside that range capture the actual event through the manual route and flag for review; do not round it or deny working-time recording. Retain non-statutory site events for **90 days after job closure** unless a documented legal/incident hold or shorter approved policy applies. If a real attendance problem later warrants a trial and no equally effective, less intrusive route exists, allow **one foreground fix per deliberate event** only at approved isolated sites with a surveyed meeting point (not adjacent, indoor or unreliable pins). Try a **100 m radius** with reported accuracy **50 m or better** and fix age **under 60 seconds**. Treat a fix whose uncertainty overlaps the boundary as inconclusive, not outside. Evaluate on device, discard coordinates immediately, and retain only a coarse check result for **30 days** if needed for corrections; no accuracy trails or customer-visible location. Bad/no fix, refusal or an outside reading cannot produce a verified-location event; leave an equal manual path and append corrections as above.

**Why:** LOPDGDD art. 90 permits employer oversight within its legal bounds and requires prior, express, clear and unequivocal information to workers and their representatives where applicable, including access, correction, restriction and erasure rights. GPS needs its own necessity/proportionality and lawful-basis review; the working-time duty and OS permission do not supply that basis, and employee consent is not a safe substitute. Establish the controller and purpose per flow; no location-based pay, sanctions or safety clearance.

**Alternatives:** No location indefinitely; a worker-entered site reference or foreman verification where confirmation of site matters.

**Evidence:** [W-06 boundary failure rules][worker], [location research][attlaw]; [LOPDGDD art. 90][geo-law] (5 December 2018), [Workers’ Statute arts. 20.3 and 20 bis][et20] (23 October 2015), [GDPR][gdpr] (27 April 2016, arts. 5–6, 12–14, 25, 28, 32, 35), [AEPD employment guide pp. 35–36, 53–54][aepd] (December 2025). The proposed radius and retention are hypotheses; a lawful need and usable field accuracy are [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Provider controller, workers/representatives and Spanish privacy counsel; approve notice, basis, access, deletion and DPIA where required before enabling any location processing, and check fixes at real sites.

**Confidence:** High.

### 17. Should we use geofenced reminders or Lock Screen shift status?

**Question:** What remains to prove for reminders, Live Activities, device lifecycle and privacy? [Attendance research][attdesign], [architecture][arch]; W-01, W-04, W-06, W-09 and OS surfaces.

**Suggested answer:** Start with optional time-based reminders. If device proof passes, show generic status after explicit attendance (iOS locally started Live Activity, ordinary ongoing Android notification); label it “Since check-in,” never “hours worked.” Link to authenticated W-06, never act from the Lock Screen. Show no address, owner name, access detail or safety all-clear; expiry, dismissal or failed push has no operational effect. Prefer local updates and no remote start until the push spike proves the signed Expo/APNs token and lifecycle path. Leave background geofencing off. If manual/scheduled reminders prove insufficient and counsel/store approval permits it, test one opt-in, arrival-only, device-local prompt for one confirmed assignment, activated by the worker in a short approved window (try **30 minutes**, no pre-work monitoring unless expressly cleared). Remove it on expiry, completion, cancellation, opt-out, sign-out or lost permission. Require proof the OS stops monitoring even if suspended; dropping callbacks is not enough. No crossing upload, persistent raw fix, payroll/attendance inference or safety decision. If the platform-work classification applies, art. 7 restricts automated data collection when a person is not offering or performing work; a booked shift or switch does not by itself satisfy that test.

**Why:** A reminder that fails in a dead spot must not damage a time record. The research identifies lifecycle, background permission, Play core-function and review risks without showing that BookBuddy's devices or build pass them.

**Alternatives:** Ship only time-based reminders and the app; no Lock Screen or geofence widgets if permission, power or lifecycle proof fails.

**Evidence:** [Device gates][attdesign] are proposed, not run: target **18/20** eligible arrivals correct within **3 minutes per OS**, at most **1/20** unrelated drive-bys, no duplicate prompt or unauthorised collection/mutation, and **≤2 percentage points** incremental drain on a matched **8-hour** screen-off run. Exercise permission loss, weak signal, neighbouring sites, OS kill vs user force-stop, reboot and offline replay separately, and keep the manual path usable in all of them. [Apple ActivityKit][activitykit], [Apple Core Location][corelocation] and [App Review §§2.5.4, 5.1.1, 5.1.5][apple-review]; [Android geofencing][android-geo], [Google Play permissions policy][play] (platform docs consulted November 2026); [Directive 2024/2831 arts. 7–11][pwd] (23 October 2024). Device, Expo, approval and off-window enforcement results are [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Spanish labour/privacy counsel and worker representatives, named release owner, App Store/Play review and signed real-device trials. A failure leaves background permission off.

**Confidence:** High.

## Later categories (not in the construction release)

### 18. Who qualifies to deliver or cover a later service?

**Question:** Which provider routes and credentials carry to later categories, and who grants category eligibility? [Design review FB2/L2][staffing], [company brief][brief]; P-04, F-07, F-09–F-12, W-03, L-02, L-05, L-06.

**Suggested answer:** Bring over the booking-coordination relationship, not the construction workforce pool. Each category starts with separately approved providers who contract for and run their own service; allow cover only from their own verified staff by the accountable coordinator, and no cover for a solo provider by default. A named local compliance lead grants **BookBuddy eligibility**, not the underlying qualification: check it with the competent authority/issuer, obtain insurer confirmation and have the provider verify site fit and actual delivery language. For cleaning/turnover use one legitimate cleaning business with its own team; no placement into household employment without separate review. For Pilates/coaching confirm the actual regulated function and applicable Valencian qualification, declaration/census and insurance before listing; exclude minors until safeguarding is approved. For massage, start with non-clinical relaxation only, not treatment or physiotherapy claims. For tours use a responsible operator/qualified guide and check route-specific authorisation, especially services at covered cultural sites. Keep external offers off unless a local reviewer clears a named route in that market and category; no reuse of a construction credential.

**Why:** The same roster controls do not make a guide, therapist and construction worker interchangeable. Category approval should follow the real work and worker relationship, not the translated listing name.

**Alternatives:** Pilot a single named provider without substitutes; later approve a genuine separate provider outcome or formal staffing route case by case.

**Evidence:** [Later screens][later], [market category research][categories], [Airbnb and staffing profiles][players]; [Valencian Ley 2/2022][sport] (22 July 2022) and [sport census procedure][sport-reg], [Ley 44/2003 art. 7.2(b)][health] (21 November 2003), [LO 8/2021 art. 57][children] (4 June 2021), and [Valencian guide procedure][guide] (Decreto 62/1996, 25 March 1996, as amended; current procedure references Ley 3/2026, 29 June 2026). Applicability to particular Pilates, coaching, massage and tour offers is [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Valencian category specialists/authorities, insurers, provider and local compliance lead; run the same clearance separately for every market/category.

**Confidence:** Medium.

### 19. What happens to bookings when a host drops out?

**Question:** When do we pause/reopen bookings, is consent needed to substitute, what if the customer declines, and who logs session attendance? [Design review L2/L3][later-review], [later specs][later]; L-02–L-06, F-10–F-12, W-03, S-17, C-12, C-17.

**Suggested answer:** Pause new bookings and prevent new confirmations for the **affected slot** as soon as required coverage is lost and no equivalent host is confirmed; existing bookings stay in force pending a decision. The provider's named coordinator verifies and confirms a current, equivalently qualified substitute; reopen once the provider has verified host, venue, language, capacity and staff/participant safety, and the offer accurately reflects the delivery terms. An equivalent substitution (unchanged provider, scope, price, venue, advertised language, time and accessibility, with no guaranteed host) needs notice, not fresh consent. A changed venue, language, time, guaranteed host, provider or other material booked term needs an explicit customer decision for the exact proposal; a different contracting provider requires a new agreement, not a roster update. On refusal, retain the original terms if deliverable; if not, offer a voluntary alternative or use the approved provider cancellation/refund path. Refusal itself is not a cancellation or charge, and staff confirmation is not customer consent. Only the designated provider host/coordinator logs actual attendance (including no-show only after checking); a past time or a booked place is not review eligibility.

**Why:** This separates staffing risk from seat count, consumer agreement and payment. The provider can stop selling an uncertain session without treating existing guests as cancelled or as having accepted new terms.

**Alternatives:** No substitutions for selected high-trust or named-host services; keep the slot closed and contact attendees if the approved replacement conditions cannot be met.

**Evidence:** [Later L-02/L-05/L-06][later], [core later-category flow][flows], [Airbnb Services roster precedent][players]. Specific grounds for cancellation/refunds depend on the approved consumer terms and mandatory rights [unverified].

**Confirm with:** Category operations, consumer counsel and the commercial owner; rehearse an equivalent substitution, a refusal of a material change and an attendance correction.

**Confidence:** High.

## Decide first

1. Sign off the provider-direct model and legal launch checklist: one named contractor per repair, no worker dispatch or BookBuddy-managed construction.
2. Select the first roughly five verified providers, the approved repairs and the eligibility/expiry reviewer. Keep outside individual cover off.
3. Staff the provider duty lines and local desk; approve the no-hold schedule and timed own-team cover rules before taking visits.
4. Approve the human briefing and teach-back protocol and secure named language cover before assigning those tasks.
5. Keep the employer's working-time register separate and launch deliberate, location-free attendance with a worker-visible correction route.
