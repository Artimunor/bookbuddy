# Design review: flows, handoffs and inventory

**Two blockers for design sign-off:** F-15 changes the original wording of an already approved quote; W-09 tells a worker to wait for verified understanding but leaves out the worker’s required acknowledgement. Neither needs a new screen. Fix the commercial record and the safety cue before using these sequences as implementation examples.

## Findings by owner

Each finding has one fix owner. A spec key plus line number identifies the file in the table above. **Blocker** means the design can communicate unsafe work or consent to a different record; **major** means a material flow, access boundary or decision can be wrong or incomplete; **minor** means the correction is local and the intended outcome is independently specified.

### DesignCustomerA

- **FL-01 · major — Published quote details disappear at customer review.** FA:61–65 includes original cost labels (`Mano de obra`, `Rodillos`) and reviewed evidence in the version being published. CA:106–114 and `C-06-quote-and-scope.svg` show only the English cost labels and no way to inspect that version’s attachments before C-07. **Fix:** bind C-06’s cost rows and customer-visible attachments to the issued version. Show the translated description and exact original where different, the same amount once, and an S-15 read-only preview returning to that quote. Exclude internal evidence explicitly at F-04; do not silently omit evidence published to the customer.
- **FL-02 · minor — A warm notification loses its parent on quote review.** CA:106,180 sends Back from C-06 to job detail even when opened from the live S-09 inbox; SA:120,124 and IA’s deep-link contract distinguish an existing caller from a constructed cold-start parent. **Fix:** warm S-09 → C-06 → Back restores the inbox filter and position; use C-05 behind C-06 on cold launch or when entered from the job. C-07 Cancel still returns to the exact C-06 version.

### DesignCustomerB

- **FL-03 · minor — Job-day routes hard-code the wrong Back destination.** CB:14,18,29,101,162–164 sends C-09 to C-10 or C-15 but says Back on either returns to C-05; it also gives C-09 a fixed C-05 return after an S-09 entry. CB:189 similarly draws C-17 dismissal to the job even when an existing schedule opened it. **Fix:** pop to the actual authorised caller on Back/Close, restoring the trigger. Construct C-05 only for a cold job link. Label C-17 dismissal separately from a confirmed transition and restore C-08 or L-05 when they opened it.
- **FL-04 · minor — The published crew change loses its time.** `C-09-job-day--crew-replaced.svg`:31 says only “Confirmed on Tue, 24 Nov”. CB:20 requires update time and date; `F-16-shift-detail.svg` records replacement confirmation and the customer update at 08:47. **Fix:** show “Crew confirmed Tue, 24 Nov · 08:47 Spain time” from the roster event (optional local equivalent as configured). Keep the unchanged arrival window separate; do not call this a read receipt.
- **FL-05 · major — A session issue is routed to a job-only form.** L:71,113 sends L-05 → C-16. CB:115–121 accepts an authorised *job*, offers “Whole job”, and opens job/payment context; the session booking has neither a construction property nor an approved repair item. **Fix:** specify a later, booking-scoped C-16 mode keyed to the session booking, provider and affected service/payment. Offer “Whole booking”, restrict case evidence to its audience and return to L-05. No lookup or fallback through an unrelated construction job.
- **FL-06 · major — The change approver cannot inspect supporting evidence.** FB:101–104 and `F-15-propose-job-change.svg` let Mateo add evidence to the change. CB:101–109 and `C-15-review-a-job-change.svg` have no evidence row, access rule or viewer route. **Fix:** identify which evidence is included when publishing a change; for issued customer-visible evidence add a received-attachments list on that exact C-15 version and read-only S-15 → same comparison. Label private draft evidence as excluded before publish. Do not let a new upload silently change what Claire approved.

### DesignForemanA

- **FL-07 · major — A booked-job reassignment lands in new-job scoping.** P:31–37 allows the incumbent to remain accountable until the incoming foreman accepts a booked job in F-03, with scope, schedule and crew intact. FA:45–51,141 offers only “Accept for scoping” → F-04. Its “no conflicting owner” rule does not distinguish the expected incumbent from a competing transfer. **Fix:** give F-03 a reassignment mode: show the current stage/owner, review the named transfer, recheck the expected incumbent and handoff version, and open F-05 on confirmed acceptance without resetting the job. Keep F-04 only for initial scoping; decline/expiry leaves the incumbent in place where still authorised.
- **FL-08 · major — The same dropout moves almost a day when Mateo taps Fill shift.** FA:3,15–21 and `F-01-today--gap-alert.svg`:37 place Nadia’s BB-1042 dropout on Mon, 23 Nov at 09:27 and call the visit “tomorrow”. Its destination `F-10-fill-shift.svg`:33 (FB:34,49) records the same dropout Tue, 24 Nov at 08:32, 28 minutes before the shift. **Fix:** use the Tuesday 08:32 event on the alert and update the day, “today/tomorrow”, agenda and urgency together. Preserve the separate Monday confirmed-crew default.

### DesignForemanB

- **FL-09 · blocker — The approved original changes at F-15.** `F-15-propose-job-change.svg`:35–50 changes approved Spanish `sustituir los rodillos` to `cambiar los rodillos` and drops the glass exclusion from the before comparison. Its new original is `Cambiar la placa del pestillo desgastada.`; `C-15-review-a-job-change.svg`:29–40 presents `Sustituir la placa de cierre desgastada.` as the original of Change 1. `F-04-scope-and-quote-editor.svg`, `C-06-quote-and-scope.svg` and CA:108 agree on the approved original. **Fix:** copy the *entire* issued v1 scope and exclusion into the locked before block: `Ajustar la puerta de la terraza y sustituir los rodillos. No incluye la sustitución del vidrio.` Use one authored addition verbatim on F-15/C-15 (for this fixture use C-15’s sentence); derive translations from it. If an author actually revises a published proposal, issue a new version and require fresh review. The matching euro totals do not repair different source terms.
- **FL-10 · minor — “First valid reply” includes a decline.** FB:36,40 and `F-11-vetted-pool--offers-pending.svg` say the first valid *reply* goes to confirmation. That very example shows Óscar declining at 08:45 before Alba accepts at 08:46 on `F-12-confirm-replacement.svg`. The intended server ordering is first valid **acceptance**. **Fix:** change the F-11 notice, F-12 badge/backplate variants and FB table to “First valid acceptance reserves the place for foreman confirmation”. A decline resolves only that worker’s offer; neither a decline nor a chat reply reserves anything.
- **FL-11 · minor — Two documented exits from the site log have no control.** FB:101,110,158,168 names F-13 as an origin for both F-15 and F-16, but FB:83–86 and `F-13-site-log.svg` map the visible actions only to safety, media, sharing and completion. **Fix:** add a labelled “View shift” row for the selected shift and “Propose change” for an authorised job (a named overflow action is sufficient). Describe the return to the same log position; don’t repurpose a photo, attendance or safety row as the trigger.

### DesignWorker

- **FL-12 · blocker — W-09 suggests work can start one step too early.** `W-09-site-briefing.svg`:14 says “Do not start the affected work until understanding is confirmed.” On `S-11-safety-instruction.svg`, “Understanding verified” already appears **before** Nadia taps “Acknowledge version 1”. W:100, SB:38–42 and core flow 5 require both events. **Fix:** say “Do not start the affected work. Mateo must verify your understanding and you must acknowledge the current instruction online.” Keep the hold until the server records that worker/version; continue to display any separate instruction to wait for Mateo’s site clearance. Attendance remains possible and does not clear work.
- **FL-13 · major — A provisionally accepted worker has no matching withdrawal or review-timeout path.** FB:40,74 explicitly allows a reserved acceptance to be withdrawn or expire at the *foreman* review cutoff. W:39–41,57–59 defines only declining an offer, dropping a **confirmed** shift, expiry at the worker reply deadline, or another worker filling it. A worker who accepted at 08:46 should not be told their offer expired at 08:50 while 08:52 review is still open. **Fix:** in W-03 show “Accepted, awaiting foreman confirmation”, the recorded review cutoff if supplied, and no travel/access. Specify W-05 “Withdraw acceptance” for a live provisional place; recheck online, return to W-03 with the recorded result, and refresh F-10/F-12. On review timeout or failed final recheck, distinguish “Not assigned” from declined, filled or a confirmed-shift dropout. Reconcile a simultaneous confirmation before offering the appropriate next action.

### DesignSharedA

- **FL-14 · major — First-time setup drops non-invitation destinations in the prose.** SA:44,56 and diagram lines 145–159 disagree: after saving S-04 the text sends non-invite users to a root, whereas the diagram and IA require resolving the retained opaque destination. A signed-out user following a permitted job/message link can finish setup somewhere unrelated. **Fix:** after confirmed setup run the same one-time resolver as S-03: matched invite → S-05, authorised record → correct parent and record, different granted context → S-07, inaccessible → S-16. Use the role root only when there is no destination.
- **FL-15 · major — “Limited role state” has no concrete worker destination.** SA:70,74,153 sends an accepted but eligibility-pending invite to “Limited role state or P-04”. P-04 is an admin surface; W:19–21 does not define a restricted new member state and ordinary empty Today means no shifts/offers, not blocked eligibility. **Fix:** resolve this case explicitly to S-16’s existing pending-eligibility mode in Worker context, with confirmed membership, work still blocked, safe contact and Back to a permitted limited/root view. Show P-04 only to a separately authorised admin in that context. Do not send the worker into offers or quietly show “no work”.
- **FL-16 · minor — The ordinary assignment receipt precedes the booking it follows.** `S-09-notifications.svg`:47 shows Nadia’s shift confirmed Mon at 08:30; `C-05-job-detail-and-timeline.svg` and `F-05-job-control.svg` book the work visit at 08:36. FA:3 explicitly orders the ordinary offer and confirmation *after* booking; Nadia’s first reference-chat message is 08:39. **Fix:** put the ordinary shift receipt after booking and before that message (e.g. 08:38 Spain time). Keep the separate cover-offer variant and briefing alert distinct.

### DesignSharedB

- **FL-17 · major — A private briefing clarification can go to the entire crew.** SB:25,36 routes “Ask Mateo to clarify” from a named worker/version to an S-10 Crew only draft. IA:185 defines Crew only by job-thread membership, not by that instruction’s recipient list; a different assigned worker in the thread can be outside the briefing audience. W:98–100 also separates clarification from acknowledgement. **Fix:** let the worker send a private clarification on S-11 to the issuing foreman for that version, or use a verified contact path with that audience. Use S-10 only when the thread is permitted for the content and all recipients are entitled; never copy the instruction, explain-back or acknowledgement into a broader crew or customer thread. Keep “not sent” explicit offline.
- **FL-18 · minor — Shared media’s caller list omits actual evidence owners.** SB:77,92,148–152 omits F-04/F-15 from S-14, and F-03/F-04/F-14/F-15/C-16 from direct S-15 entry. FA:49,63, FB:94,103 and CB:119 explicitly use them. S-15’s “Ask Mateo in chat” example cannot be applied to a foreman reviewing Claire’s intake or to a private support case. **Fix:** list the missing callers with their record, audience, allowed media/edit actions, and exact return target. Submitted evidence is read-only; unsent draft owner actions stay with that draft. Offer chat clarification only for a permitted message in the same thread. Keep S-14 restricted by caller capability: an admin’s P-02 job-thread grant does not by itself grant capture/upload.
- **FL-19 · minor — The confirmation dialog’s route table omits real callers and admits the wrong P-02 result.** SB:118–122,157–158 lists five origins; CA:110, FA:49,63,93,109, FB:50,64,85,94,103, SA:74 and L:85,117 also use S-17. Its handoff example says the result may be “offered or assigned”, while P:35 says P-02 sends an offer only. **Fix:** document the caller-specific review and return targets for C-06 decline; F-03/04/06/07/10/11/13/14/15; S-05 decline; and later L-06. Include C-15 decline as well as approve. For P-02 require “Send handoff offer · awaiting [foreman]”; only subsequent F-03 acceptance can transfer ownership. Cancel restores the initiating control/selection; show the authoritative result on its own parent. Keep C-07, F-12 and S-11 as dedicated decisions.
- **FL-20 · minor — Sign-off replaces the page behind the dialog.** In `S-17-confirm-consequential-action.svg`:25–47 the visible backing page switches to “Ready for your review” and “Sign off on work”; the actual `C-10-completion-and-sign-off.svg` uses “Awaiting sign-off” and the trigger “Review sign-off”, with the two approved items and “After adjustment” evidence. **Fix:** use the actual reviewed C-10 state under the scrim. Keep “Confirm sign-off” inside the dialog, restore focus to “Review sign-off” on Cancel, and change C-10 to Signed off only on receipt.
- **FL-21 · minor — The account reading preference is labelled chat-only.** `S-12-language-and-display.svg` and SB:52–56 call the control “Chat translation” / “Reading language for messages”; CA:10 and CB:10 use the reading preference for quotes and changes as well, and W-09/S-11 display translated instructions. **Fix:** label the account control “Reading language” and describe the surfaces it affects (including messages, work documents and instructions). Keep app language, declared work/briefing language and source text independent. If a chat-only override is approved later, label it separately.
- **FL-22 · major — The later studio conversation has no receiving audience contract.** L:71–73,112 sends L-05 to S-10’s “session audience” with Marina as contact. SB:19–25,64–68 and IA:183–187 define Job conversation/Crew only and their participants; neither says how the session booking and coordinator replace a construction job and foreman. Do not enrol other attendees, expose crew-only content or add a launch audience/tab. Apply the same scope to S-14/S-15 attachments.

### DesignLater

- **FL-24 · major — Material session substitutions have no customer decision path.** L:73 requires explicit consent to a venue, advertised-language, time or guaranteed-host change; L:85–87 lets the provider propose a change or use S-17. Its diagram offers Claire only chat, case, review, or C-17 cancel/reschedule. - **FL-25 · major — A failed reschedule is described as no booking.** L:51 distinguishes a failed new booking from a failed reschedule, and L:59–61 preserves the existing pass.
