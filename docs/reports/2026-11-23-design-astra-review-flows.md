### AstraLead

- **AF-01 · minor — The gallery still describes superseded flows.** `README.md:25–102` embeds 114 of the 171 screen SVGs. F-17, W-10 and S-19 have no primary gallery entry; C-11’s failed *report* is captioned “payment failed”, W-06’s outside-*time* variant is captioned “outside geofence”, and the individual F-11 captions/alt text omit “later” despite the section-level warning. A reader using those labels gets the wrong operating model. **Fix:** rebuild the gallery from the current inventory and spec illustrations, using their semantic titles, not inherited filenames. Label F-11 entries later-only individually; retain the stable file paths. The source art is present.
- **AF-02 · minor — The Follow prompt names the destination, not its contents.** `C-18-follow-live.svg:69` says only “Show visit status on your Lock Screen” immediately below an authenticated record containing the property and the foreman’s name. `system/design-system.md` (Follow control), CB §C-18 and SB §S-18 require explaining **what will appear before** the tap. The actual S-18 projection is appropriately redacted. **Fix:** replace the helper with e.g. “Show the visit phase and report time on your Lock Screen. No names or address.” Keep Follow explicit, optional and foreground-only; use the Android surface name on Android.
- **AF-03 · minor — The inventory asks for a notification claim the worker cannot verify.** `screens/inventory.md:62` lists “foreman notified” between sending and failure. W:68–74 correctly defines a dropout receipt, queued notice, indeterminate result and **Foreman not notified** on failed send, but no affirmative human-delivery state. F-10 receiving the staffing gap is not Mateo reading the alert. **Fix:** replace the inventory state with “dropout recorded; delivery/read not confirmed”. If a separately evidenced delivery or human acknowledgement is surfaced, name its source and meaning explicitly. Keep the immediate duty-call path for a known failed urgent send.

### AstraCustomerA

- **AF-04 · minor — A proposed window arrives without its response deadline.** FA:109–113 requires an absolute proposal expiry at F-06 review, capped by the next staffed day or safe staffing cutoff. `C-08-schedule-a-visit.svg`, `C-08-schedule-a-visit--assessment.svg` and CA:148–158 show the appointment interval and handle an *already expired* choice, but supply no place to see when the current proposal expires. The appointment start is not the expiry. **Fix:** carry the exact server deadline into each pending proposal; show “Reply by [site date, time, zone]” next to the unreserved window and include it in the accessible choice. Do not turn it into a slot hold or a running countdown.
- **AF-05 · major — The early-start confirmation does not show what Claire acknowledged.** `C-08-schedule-a-visit.svg:38–45,56` shows a checked “I request an early start”, “Within my withdrawal period” and a terms link. No visible choice, review summary or statement records acknowledgement of the applicable withdrawal consequences. CA:150–152 explicitly distinguishes the selected request from the required acknowledgement; [commercial decision §8](2026-11-23-open-question-suggestions/commercial.md#8-repair-cancellations-and-revised-visits) requires both where applicable. The checked state is *not* by itself a defect or evidence of preselection. **Fix:** show the consequence alongside the express request, or show a reviewed acknowledgement tied to the exact terms before enabling this early window. Make clear that starting alone does not extinguish withdrawal; retain a later-window path and an initially unticked decision. Have counsel approve the production wording.

### AstraCustomerB

- **AF-06 · major — The customer and publisher use different change-expiry rules.** CB:118 calls the response window “72 hours at most”; FB §F-15 and [commercial decision §7](2026-11-23-open-question-suggestions/commercial.md#7-scope-and-change-approval) make 72 hours the **default**, capped by an earlier start of the affected work. A published deadline later than 72 hours, with no earlier work start, would still be valid at F-15 but rejected under C-15’s rule. **Fix:** bind display and approval to the actual issued deadline and current server disposition. Describe the same default/cap on both sides; never shorten the customer’s deadline independently.

### AstraSharedB

- **AF-14 · major — The consequential-action table omits provider operations with different effects.** SB:184–185 supports P-02 **send handoff offer** and P-05 **revoke access**. P §P-02 explicitly invokes S-17 to *withdraw* a handoff and to *issue a locked commercial version*; P:98–104 invokes it to *withdraw an unaccepted invitation* or confirm an immediate access change. Sending a handoff does not issue a quote; withdrawing an invitation must not revoke a membership that just became active. **Fix:** split the rows by operation. Bind issue to the locked quote/change, publisher grant, named approver, exact terms and evidence. Bind handoff withdrawal to the pending offer (incumbent unchanged); invitation withdrawal to the still-unconsumed link (if acceptance won, re-review, never auto-revoke); immediate membership edits to the exact grant/effective expiry. Cancel restores the caller’s selection; show only the authoritative result on P-02/P-03.
- **AF-15 · minor — S-17 promises a different return after successful publication.** FB §F-15 says confirmed issue returns to F-05 with **Open change**; SB:182 keeps the user on F-15. Similarly FA:105 allows exiting F-06 to its caller with **Proposal sent**, but SB:176 specifies the F-06 date/agenda after the send and makes expiry optional. These yield different back stacks for the same decisions. **Fix:** distinguish Cancel from committed success in the shared table/diagrams: Cancel restores the exact F-06/F-15 editor; on successful change issue follow FB to F-05 and link back to the issued proposal, with withdrawal shown against its own version. On schedule send show the provider spec’s caller/result path and the issued proposal deadline. Never return to a customer-role screen on the sender’s device.

## Consolidated open design questions

The nine specs contain **27 source bullets** (three each); some ask several questions and FA3 is a fixture clarification, not a decision. The [plan](../../design-astra/foundation/improvement-plan.md) has no separate “Open design questions” section. Its explicit deferred decisions and validation conditions are folded in below. Keys such as **CB2** mean the second bullet in that spec’s question section. The defects above are not questions for the owner to decide again.

### Market, local coverage and staffing

- **Where and at what scale will the pilot run, and for how long?** Which catchment/address service and named local contact resolve ambiguous addresses? Xàbia is a fixture, not an adopted pilot. **CA1; plan, Decisions.**
- **Who actually covers the duty route for each booked visit?** Name the verified provider work line, duty lead, backup, local desk and staffed hours; set the shift’s safe staffing cutoff and rehearse a missed alert/dropout, unanswered call and failed confirmation. Who handles emergency transfers and eligibility corrections by an independently verified route? Do not publish an untested callback time. **FA1, FB1, W3, SB2, P2.**
- **Who owns an open case in that catchment?** Name handler and backup, plus the appropriate regional complaints/ADR route. Distinguish a response target from guaranteed cover. **CB1.**

### Commercial terms, records and property authority

- **Who reviews, publishes and supports the provider’s commercial work?** Name each provider’s reviewer, authorised publishing backup and billing contact, plus BookBuddy’s contact for disputed fee lines. Who confirms the issued invoice series, VAT treatment and correction references with the issuer and tax adviser? The cohort, €25 fee and qualifying conditions are settled trial terms, not a proven price point. **FA1, P1, P3; plan, Fee.**
- **Who approves the binding customer wording?** Identify provider signatory and consumer-law reviewer for each required contracting language; validate consent, express early performance, applicable acknowledgement, withdrawal and exception disclosures. Do not reopen provider-direct billing or fee-free eligible cancellation. **CA2, CB2.**
- **What redacted proof will operations accept for property transfer, and when will it be deleted?** Retain the independently verified successor requirement; an invite or pin is not proof of authority. **CB2.**
- **What evidence does this particular shift/approved scope need?** Decide with the provider where a photograph rather than a note or agreed alternative is required. Selection, successful upload and sharing are different events. **W3.**

### Language, human safety and comprehension

- **Who will review European Portuguese and the reading-language directions?** Cover customer, onboarding, field, accessibility and private push copy, later class/access/booking terms, and readable English/Portuguese live-report labels on small devices. Which authored → reading directions pass field quality and privacy review for intake, messages and issued-document aids? Keep Original only and the two launch UI catalogs independent. **CA3, FA2, FB3, W2, SA1, SB1, L2; plan, Language.**
- **Who can actually lead and verify the human briefing for each task, spoken language or dialect?** Name a competent briefer/interpreter and backup; have the prevention lead and crew rehearse the task and its time window. A UI catalog or RTL sample is not evidence of Spanish, Arabic/Darija or other human briefing coverage. **FA2, FB2, W2, SB1.**
- **Do foremen and remote customers interpret risk and corrections correctly?** Test “next report by” against an arrival promise, and whether readers can identify the last valid milestone after correction. The 08:12 dropout and 09:12 on-site fixture are independent branches, not a claim that cover succeeded. **FB3; FA3 (fixture, not an open policy); plan, Live layer.**

### Devices, identity and cross-device state

- **Which signed phones/builds pass the display gates?** Test customer and worker local start, verified updates, stale timeout, redaction/teardown after scope loss or switch, denial, dismissal, eight-hour expiry and unlock-to-W-06; include non-Island iPhone and Android API 24. If remote updates fail, keep only proven local status; without a proven local path use the in-app record. Remote start is not a launch decision to revisit here. **CB3, W1, SB3; plan, Follow by choice.**
- **Can people complete enrolled-passkey recovery and privileged step-up accessibly on the supported mobile devices and owner web?** Until proved, use independent verification for privileged operations, not weaker email/phone proof. **SA2.**
- **What does a second offline device show about already-read messages or alerts?** The question is stale presentation, not the accepted read rule: per-audience message positions and individual alert reads, monotonic merge after reauthorisation, and no read/acknowledgement from a preview or transport receipt. **SA3.**

### Later sessions

- **Which studio, insurer and local compliance lead verify the actual instructor function, venue access and emergency provision in the first enabled market?** A step-free fixture does not certify a venue or choose a market. **L1.**
- **Can bookers distinguish a ten-minute hold from a booking, and can bookers and coordinators complete both change paths at maximum text size with a screen reader?** Check that the original time and party stay identifiable throughout. Test in the reviewed terminology; pay at venue, hold duration and fee-free cancellation are not open questions. **L2, L3.**
