# Astra visual review before export

**16 findings: 0 blockers, 8 major, 8 minor.** Both renderers produced all 172 artboards. The most consequential problems are in the fixtures rather than the rendering engines: a named offer has two deadlines, enabled actions outlive their cutoffs, and a worker’s photo documents a repair while her shift card still says not to start it. The customer’s Lock Screen opt-in also omits the preview its own specification requires.

## AstraLead — 1 major, 2 minor

- **AV-01 · major — Follow live does not say what will become visible while locked.** [`C-18-follow-live.svg`, `follow-control`](../../design-astra/screens/customer/C-18-follow-live.svg); `C-09_C-17-specs.md` §C-18; `system/design-system.md` §Visit ticket and live components. Name the notification instead on Android. Keep opening the ledger independent of following.
- **AV-02 · minor — The master template’s completed ticks disappear into the rail.** [`system/wireframe-template.svg`, `visit-ticket`](../../design-astra/system/wireframe-template.svg); compare `C-18-follow-live.svg`. Both renders show small cuts in the first two segments rather than C-18’s legible checked marks. The template also puts the report source *after* the rail, contrary to the drawing convention. **Fix:** copy the completed mint circles and contrasting ticks from C-18, keeping the current diamond and future dash. Put the source/time immediately below **Crew on site** and before the rail; move the repair/property context to the context row.
- **AV-03 · minor — The compact Island touches the cellular icon.** [`S-18-live-activity--compact.svg`, `system-status` and `dynamic-island-compact`](../../design-astra/screens/shared/S-18-live-activity--compact.svg). The capsule spans x=89–301 and the first signal bar starts at x=301 in the same vertical band. Both renders make the bar look attached to the capsule; the minimal state has clear separation. **Fix:** keep the capsule centred with bounds x=97–293, move the leading glyph and trailing phase inward by eight units, and retain the empty sensor region. This leaves eight units before the signal group.
