# Authorization review — 2026-11-24 (independent reviewer)

**Release recommendation: hold.** HEAD ref: `47fafcbc84ad6a117686f958a1b8a1bfb7f61d04`. Paths below are relative to `apps/api/src/` unless noted.

**High severity**

1. **Customer sign-off can issue a bill without a billing issuer.** `modules/aftercare/completion.ts:265–286,346–373`. A foreman without `issue_bill` can supply a bill reference and matching amount; the customer’s sign-off then creates an issued invoice and due date. The existing test explicitly expects this with `canIssueBill: false` (`apps/api/test/aftercare-completion.test.ts:342–368`). Keep the reference as a hint; require an attributed provider billing grant to issue.
2. **Case stewardship uses an ordinary customer session.** `modules/aftercare/routes.billing.ts:80–83`; `modules/aftercare/cases.ts:28–32,250–255,294–304`. The configured steward is identified at case creation. Removing that configuration does not stop the former steward’s customer session from reading existing reports, disclosing provider packets, or closing cases. Require a live, independently revocable internal stewardship grant on every read and write, in addition to case assignment.
3. **Private case corrections leak through the completion view.** `modules/aftercare/cases.ts:318–323`; `modules/aftercare/completion.ts:135–137,159–161,176–179`. A correction is stored for `reporter_and_owner`, but the job foreman receives its original text, actor name and case ID without a case grant or provider packet—even when the reporter selected no provider audience. Return only the fact that the completion was returned; disclose a reason only through an explicitly shared packet.
4. **Later routes bypass rollout gates advertised by bootstrap.** `modules/auth/session.ts:80–93`; `modules/sessions/booking.ts:183–205,302–314`; `modules/sessions/hosting.ts:387–395`. An admin of a business with its Sessions flag off can create listings in an enabled market/category. A customer with no eligible place in that market can browse and hold there. Apply the same business, category and caller-capability predicates to the target in each route, including chat and sync.
5. **Dropped workers can still follow a live visit.** `modules/field/attendance.ts:344–350,373–386,392–407`; `modules/schedule/shifts.ts:89–96`. A former assignee’s membership and past confirmation timestamp still authorize detail and snapshot reads. The field response includes the visit’s *latest* work event and actor, including events after dropout. Separate the worker’s historical receipt from live visit data; require a current confirmed assignment for the latter.
6. **Linked ordinary media never acquires its retention deadline.** `modules/chat/commands.ts:119–124`; `modules/field/attendance.ts:303–308`; `modules/media/retention.ts:13–24`; `modules/media/read.ts:25–29`. Linking clears `delete_after`; the sweeper excludes linked objects without a deadline. A job reader can continue obtaining chat or progress media grants past the 180- or 90-day post-close limit. Set deadlines on close, account for all links and holds, and enforce expiry on reads independently of deletion.
7. **A clean sync delta can conceal grant expiry.** `modules/jobs/projections.ts:42–54,132–146`; `modules/sync/read.ts:49–62,74–90`. When a backup foreman’s timed job grant expires without another write, the job fails a fresh snapshot check, but the delta sees neither a changed key nor an epoch bump. The foreman’s cache receives no removal. Bind sync authorization to the next relevant grant expiry or emit a durable removal/reset when it expires.

**Medium severity**

8. **Collection can be deferred for undisputed lines.** `modules/aftercare/bills.ts:63–65,218–232`. A billing authority with a packet for a single disputed item can select unrelated invoice lines: the amount check sums the caller’s selection, not the case’s affected scope. Map the disputed scope to immutable invoice lines and bound the deferral to that amount.
9. **Cover can create an assignment after contract withdrawal.** `modules/staffing/ordinary.ts:45–49`; `modules/staffing/cover.ts:62–65,336,543–549`; `modules/commercial/notices.ts:83–95`. Ordinary staffing requires approved, unwithdrawn scope; cover confirmation checks vacancy and worker fit but not that scope. A foreman can confirm replacement access on a still-booked visit after withdrawal. Reuse the ordinary scope check at cover issue, acceptance and confirmation.
10. **Future attendance can publish “on site” immediately.** `modules/field/attendance.ts:107–115,145–160`; `modules/field/live.ts:75–84`. A confirmed worker can submit a future time inside the shift’s normal arrival window. It is accepted and published at receipt time, before the alleged arrival. Reject or send impossible future times to review without publishing; preserve legitimate delayed offline submissions.
11. **The briefing returns customer-site access details.** `modules/field/safety.ts:103–106,132–147`. A confirmed worker receives the normalized address and authored arrival plan. This is not offer-recipient access, and W‑09 explicitly expects post-confirmation directions, but it conflicts with the stated “never customer access details” rule. Separate permitted worksite directions from private access instructions and enforce that distinction in the response.
12. **Customer scheduling reveals unpublished staffing trouble.** `modules/schedule/read.ts:23–38,60–71`. The customer sees `no_crew_available` from internal vacancies and capacity even before the provider publishes a cover update or revised proposal. Derive the customer label from that published event; keep the live capacity calculation on the provider side.
13. **Session sync includes online-only access notes.** `modules/sessions/booking.ts:42–56,113–128,143–152`. The booker’s sync projection embeds the full agreement, including venue address, access note and arrival note, instead of a cache-safe summary. Give sync a separate allowlisted schema; keep access instructions on the authorized detail read.
14. **Media URLs can outlive a known grant deadline.** `modules/media/policy.ts:94–99`; `modules/media/read.ts:25–39`. An intake recipient can retain readable request-media URLs beyond the handoff’s expiry: signing caps lifetime by session and object expiry, not the authorizing grant. Return the effective authorization deadline with the checked link and cap the signature at it.
15. **Quote/change evidence caps apply per upload stage, not per version.** `modules/media/storage.ts:87–97`; `modules/commercial/commands.ts:63–72,117–125`; `packages/api-contract/src/commercial.ts:266–271`. A foreman can combine multiple valid stages into one version with up to 20 attachments, exceeding five photos plus one voice. Count actual stored media types in the final version-write guard.
16. **Voice duration and media type are attested by the uploader.** `modules/media/transfer.ts:37–45,110–115`. Settlement checks size and declared R2 metadata, not the media stream. An uploader can attach audio longer than 90 seconds while declaring a shorter duration. Verify type and duration with trusted processing before marking the object ready.
17. **Most push kinds fail open on resource revocation.** `effects/push.ts:13–41,74–83`; `modules/notifications/read.ts:8–18,32–39`. A customer whose bill access expired can still receive a queued alert and read its `bill_paid` kind in the inbox. The push body is generic and the link does not itself authorize opening the bill. Recheck the destination-specific grant at claim and inbox projection; default unknown kinds to no delivery.

**Checked and OK, apart from the defects above:** Jobs bind handoffs to the job and named recipient. Commercial customer reads exclude unpublished revisions, and author/publisher accounts cannot approve their own version. Schedule writes correlate visit, window and job. Ordinary offers bind the recipient; cover acceptance checks round/revision, reserves one winner, and does not itself assign the worker. Assisted attendance and safety acknowledgement check actor and assignment. Sign-off checks exact terms and the named approver; record-paid checks a live provider grant, bill amount and prior record. Rating text is not returned to another customer. Construction chat rechecks thread audience, assignment and history bounds; translation claims recheck eligible readers. Media reads require ready, linked objects. Sync verifies HMAC, account and context and uses a closed operation list. Receipts match account, context, operation and input hash. No request-controlled SQL syntax was identified. Push payloads contain an opaque destination, not authored text; contractual mail targets the signer’s verified mailbox.
