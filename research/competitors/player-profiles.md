# Competitor and adjacent-player profiles

Research snapshot: 21 November 2026. There are **63 core player profiles** followed by **59 adjacent, search-only or context-only entity profiles**. The latter include possible products, comparison publishers and corporate parents; their inclusion does not establish that they compete in Europe. Aliases, acquired brands and name collisions are labelled rather than counted as independent live networks.

## Reading the evidence

- `unknown` means the retained sources did not establish the field. `[unverified]` marks a weak, secondary, incomplete or conflicting assertion. No missing field has been filled from unsourced memory.
- Most evidence is an indexed extract, not a product trial. All eight attempted direct web-reader requests timed out; [sources.md](sources.md) records their status. Full retained extracts and every exact query are in [search-log.md](search-log.md).
- Country lists are minimums or explicitly historical, not proof that every omitted European country is unserved. Consumer, provider and worker UI languages are different. Translation of a website is not translation of operational chat.
- Prices and undated scale figures are snapshots encountered in 2026 unless the text gives another year. Registered workers, active workers, ratings, venues, job value, revenue and acquisition prices are not interchangeable.
- Source lists retain direct citations and related search-result URLs, including conflicting third-party comparisons. They are not a claim that every listed page proves every field. Funding unknown is not zero funding; revenue unknown is not zero revenue.
- Exact annual revenue is unknown unless explicitly reported. Personal contact details and street addresses are omitted.
