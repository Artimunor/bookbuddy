## Search limitations

- Searches with several country names sometimes returned only one country or broad reports. Absence from the result list is not evidence of a zero market.
- Repeated publications surfaced through different queries or mirrors; the inventory keeps the distinct URLs without treating them as independent corroboration.
- Generated fallback answers sometimes linked irrelevant or conflicting sources. Their numeric claims are retained as unverified discovery material, not silently converted into official data.
- Direct-read failures and binary-extraction limitations are recorded in `sources.md`; they are not successful data verification.
