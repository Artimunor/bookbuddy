# Astra improvement plan: follow the visit

Claire should be able to glance at her phone and answer three questions: **What happened? Who reported it? Do I need to do anything?** On the same job, Nadia needs a different answer: **Am I assigned, is my arrival recorded, and is any work still on hold?** Build those views from the same visit, not the same feed. The useful part of a ride-status experience is a trustworthy next step, not a moving dot.

## Keep from Sol

The existing 70 IDs already carry a repair from first request to aftercare, including the usually neglected cases: an old quote, two workers replying for one slot, an unreceived photo and a revoked invite. Keep the four role shells, the property and business scopes, the named foreman, the site’s time, the original alongside its translation and the distinction between saved, sent and confirmed. Keep the labelled tabs, expandable sheets with real parents, field-sized controls and native Back/Up. A customer abroad can use the signed-in web record without installing the app.

The rendered family reads consistently, but the hierarchy changes little between tasks. C-01 gives a request invitation more weight than a booked visit; C-09 has window, roster and contact but no history of what the crew has actually reported. W-01 separates the briefing from tomorrow’s shift without showing how to return to an active shift. F-13 already distinguishes private evidence from sharing: use that same deliberate boundary for status. S-10’s bilingual bubbles, P-02’s accountable handoff and L-03’s explicit capacity conflict are useful models; do not turn them into tracking dashboards.

## Refresh the hierarchy, not the shell

- **Active visit:** give one deep-green *visit ticket* the first useful position. Put the phase in large type and the source and site time directly below. Mint notches work like the edge of a printed service ticket: solid/check for reported, ring/diamond for current, dashed for unreported. A short text label says the same thing. No guessed percentages, maps, vehicle pictures or countdown to an unpromised arrival.
- **Quiet detail:** use a warm paper ground on visit views, the existing green actions and white evidence/decision surfaces. Replace repeated cards for adjacent facts with one ruled list. Group initials with the person’s actual responsibility; keep an issue, hold or lost connection visibly distinct from green success. Quotes lead with version and total; a form leads with the question; an empty Jobs view still leads with requesting a repair.
- **Density:** maintain 20-unit page margins, one primary decision, and a 48-unit target (56–64 on site). Allow space for a second line rather than fitting translated text into a pill. Reserve the strongest weight for the one fact the person came to find. OS surfaces get a short summary, not a miniature app page.
- **Motion and delight:** let a confirmed report settle into its notch once in 180 ms. The update’s verb, time and new shape remain after motion stops. Reduced motion uses an immediate change; a screen reader hears the update once, without losing focus. No infinite pulse, urgency animation, celebratory payment effects or haptics for an unconfirmed event.

## Add the live layer

1. **Publish, do not infer.** C-08 supplies a confirmed window; a foreman explicitly reports **On the way** or **On site** in new F-17. F-14 supplies **In review** only after submission. A private worker check-in, elapsed time, chat, a provisional offer or evidence upload never moves a customer milestone. A delay or cover problem is an annotation, not a replacement booking. Keep the last confirmed report, site time, named foreman and next update if promised. Correct visibly; never backfill missing steps.
2. **Read in the app first.** C-01/C-05/C-09 lead to the current visit in C-18. The ledger uses confirmed, audience-safe events only. Do not share worker coordinates, routes, individual attendance or reasons for absence. W-01/W-04 lead to W-10 instead: Nadia sees her personal attendance receipt and separate hold. Checkout opens W-06; no app or OS card grants permission to start work.
3. **Follow by choice.** Opening C-18 does not subscribe. **Follow live** in the foreground starts the optional local iPhone Live Activity (S-18), or an ordinary Android ongoing notification (S-19). Worker status follows a deliberate check-in and opt-in. Gracefully show the in-app record on unsupported devices, web, denial, stale delivery, dismissal or expiry. **Stop following** affects this display only. iOS permits push-to-start only on 17.2+ with a prior token; remote start is later, not an implied launch capability. At eight hours active the activity ends; its final Lock Screen snapshot can remain up to four hours more. No automatic chain. Android is not an Island emulation. Neither platform runs a network timer.

## Decisions that replace prototype assumptions

| Decision | Consequence for every slice |
| --- | --- |
| Language | UI: **English** (`en`, default) and **Português (Portugal)** (`pt-PT`), selectable before sign-in. Bundle complete ICU catalogs, enable reviewed locales through metadata. Reading preference can be Original only; reviewed translation pairs, authored language and spoken briefing language are separate. Keep Arabic content/RTL stress fixtures without offering Arabic or Spanish as a launch UI. |
| Contract and money | The named provider sells, invoices and remedies. The customer pays it directly. C-11 records a report against a bill and distinguishes it from provider-matched receipt; a screenshot, return link or sign-off is not payment. No BookBuddy checkout. The original approved quote is immutable; changes require a separately published version, named approver and explicit consent. |
| Fee | First **30 completed paid jobs**: no BookBuddy fee, not a free repair. Under new accepted paid-trial terms **€25 + applicable VAT**, billed monthly to the provider for each BookBuddy-sourced, signed-off, paid job **≥ €150 before VAT**. No leads, cancellations, provider-sourced work or fully refunded jobs; credit subsequent full refunds. No customer surcharge or worker fee. Paid-trial pricing is a hypothesis; pilot geography, size and duration are not decided. |
| Cover | Own provider crew only. First valid acceptance reserves the slot; the foreman confirms the assignment after a fresh recheck. A reservation is not permission to travel. If no cover, agree a revised visit, or resolve the old agreement and introduce a verified business for a new one. F-11, its alerts and all individual vetted-pool offers are **later**, not something a timer unlocks. |
| Cancellation, safety and privacy | Cancel/reschedule without a fee under the accepted visit rules; distinguish appointment management, work already performed and statutory withdrawal. Require human briefing, teach-back verified in a language the worker understands, **and** that worker’s acknowledgement of the current version. Deliberate W-06 attendance needs no location and is not the statutory time register. No gate or alarm codes in any field, chat, media, translation, cache or push. |

Use the [inventory](../screens/inventory.md), [live component dimensions and semantics](../system/design-system.md#visit-ticket-and-live-components) and [drawing conventions](../system/wireframe-conventions.md). Remove settled questions; retain genuine device, review or operating unknowns.
